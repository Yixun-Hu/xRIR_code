## 2026-09-14 — post-hoc diagnostic (Planner, Fable 5.1): why the cylindrical model loses on the hallway
- **Trigger** — Yixun asked for the reason behind the real-room deficit. Descriptive analysis of the existing exp_02 per-sample files plus one CPU forward-pass probe on the zero-shot checkpoints; no run artefacts touched, nothing re-evaluated, GPUs not used. Scripts in `diagnostics_2026-09-14_hallway_side/` (`hall_diag*.py`, `gt_sides.py`, `side_probe.py`; run from the repo root in the xRIR env).
- **Result** — (1) Ground truth: the HAA loudspeaker is directional and faces −y in every room; in the hallway the measured C50 is +2.3…+3.8 dB for mics at −y and −6.4…−8.2 dB for mics at +y (≈10 dB gap at every distance), total energy 2× lower at +y; 7 of the 12 training mics are at −y, 5 at +y; 185 of 423 test mics are at +y. (2) The hallway panorama at the speaker is nearly 180°-rotation symmetric (mean relative pixel difference under a 256-column roll 0.09, vs 0.57 for a 90° roll; classroom 0.14, complex 0.32, dampened 0.014). (3) Zero-shot hallway errors split by side: cyl C50 1.04 dB at −y / 8.23 dB at +y, EDT 0.144 / 0.235 s, T60 4.8 / 16.5 %; control 2.37 / 2.42 dB, 0.247 / 0.110 s, 9.3 / 5.2 %; released 1.85 / 2.41 dB. Fine-tuned (3 seeds): cyl 1.0–1.1 / 3.2–3.6 dB, control 0.8 / 1.6 dB, released 0.9 / 2.6–2.8 dB. The same sign pattern (cyl better at −y, worse at +y zero-shot) appears weakly in classroom, complex and dampened. (4) CPU probe, 24 +y and 24 −y test queries, eval_seed 0 references: the geometry-branch pooled feature (`lin_proj_0` of the ViT) of a +y mic vs its exact mirror (−x,−y) has cosine 0.975 for cyl (closer than a same-side neighbour 1.2 m away, 0.955) but 0.435 for the control and 0.325 for the released model (neighbour 0.911 / 0.808). Share of |mixing weight| placed on −y references for +y queries: cyl 0.85, control 0.24, released 0.27 (0.66 of the drawn references are −y); for −y queries 0.99 / 0.89 / 0.90. Signed spectral C50 error (pred − true) for +y queries: cyl +7.4 dB, control +1.7, released +1.9; for −y queries −0.6 / −1.9 / −1.4.
- **Analysis** — Mechanism established: the cylindrical encoder is azimuth-equivariant and its learned pool leaves the geometry feature nearly invariant (exp_03: 1.7 % change under a one-patch roll), so in a panorama that is itself 180°-symmetric the branch cannot separate the two ends of the corridor; the reference attention then selects references by distance regardless of end and, with a −y majority in every draw, predicts a −y-like RIR for +y mics. Real rooms break the symmetry the encoder was built to ignore, through the directional loudspeaker, which no geometry input represents. The SimpleViT's absolute positional encoding and unrotated xyz separate the ends and pick same-side references. Fine-tuning on 12 RIRs teaches the shared coordinate branch part of the side information (cyl +y error 8.2 → 3.2–3.6 dB) but not enough to match the control (1.6 dB). This is a descriptive diagnosis on the realised runs (not pre-registered, 24 queries per side in the probe, unadjusted); it explains the hallway cells and is consistent with the smaller classroom/complex deficits, which it does not quantify separately.
- **Next** — none scheduled. If the deficit matters for the method: (a) an azimuth-invariant pool would make the ambiguity total, an absolute-azimuth channel or source-orientation input would remove it; (b) any HAA yaw test must rotate the loudspeaker orientation together with the geometry.

## 2026-09-14 — oracle probe: ceiling of a side-aware fix (Planner, Fable 5.1; CPU, zero-shot checkpoints, hallway)
- **Trigger** — Yixun asked whether the cylindrical model can be made to beat the SimpleViT on the real-room benchmark. Script `diagnostics_2026-09-14_hallway_side/oracle_probe.py`; 16 test queries per corridor side; spectral proxies (C50 from the predicted magnitude spectrogram at the geometric onset, log-spectrogram L1 against the target), not the waveform metrics.
- **Result** — Restricting references to the query's own side (K = 5 at +y, K = 7 at −y) removes the cylindrical model's +y failure: spectral C50 error 6.60 → 0.99 dB (control 1.92 → 1.04, released 2.12 → 1.09); at −y 1.39 → 1.30 (control 2.78 → 1.35). Under the same-side oracle the cylindrical model has the lowest log-spectrogram L1 in both cells (0.638 vs control 0.653 at +y; 0.618 vs 0.663 at −y), i.e. the encoder itself is at least as good as the SimpleViT once the end-of-corridor ambiguity is resolved. Other-side-only references give ≈ 9–10 dB error for every model, confirming the directivity gap is what is being mis-predicted.
- **Analysis** — The deficit is entirely in reference selection driven by the azimuth-blind geometry feature, not in the encoder's representation of the room. The achievable gain on HAA as released is therefore parity or a small (few %) loss/EDT advantage, not a decisive win: each HAA room has one fixed loudspeaker heading, so the SimpleViT's absolute-azimuth prior is the right prior for this benchmark. Descriptive, 16 queries per side, unadjusted.
- **Next** — decision for Yixun: (A) pretraining fix = orientation-relative azimuth channel in the cylindrical encoder (joint scene+orientation equivariance; ~30–40 h per model, GPUs blocked by FLAC exp_13); (B) fine-tune-only fix = explicit angle-to-speaker-axis feature on the query/reference coordinate tokens, applied to both backbones (6 jobs × ~50 min); (C) protocol variant = orientation-aware reference selection for all models. Any variant is a new pre-registered experiment (exp_06) with the fix applied symmetrically to the control.
