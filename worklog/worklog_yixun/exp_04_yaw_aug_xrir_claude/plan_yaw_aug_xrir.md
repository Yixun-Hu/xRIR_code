## 1. Question

Does training-time random yaw augmentation — one physically consistent yaw per training sample, the contract of the user's FLAC exp_17 arm (`/home/yixunhu/codespace/exp-17-yawaug-a6000`, `training.yaw_aug = {enabled, img_w: 512, seed}`) — change xRIR's few-shot RIR prediction on AcousticRooms (a) at the canonical heading and (b) under scene yaw, relative to the same-budget un-augmented SimpleViT control of exp_01? ## 2. Treatment and single-delta contract (port of exp_17 §2)

- **Augmentation semantics (port of FLAC `draw_yaw_offsets` / `offsets_to_radians` / `rotate_scene_metadata` and `_apply_yaw_aug`):** for every training sample, one integer panorama-column offset `d ~ Uniform{0, …, W−1}`, W = 512 (every sample rotated, no skip probability), exact angle `Δ = 2πd/W`, active yaw of the whole scene: `depth_coord' = Rz(Δ)·roll(depth_coord, d, dim=W)`, `src_loc' = Rz(Δ)·src_loc`, `ref_locs' = Rz(Δ)·ref_locs`. The target RIR and the reference RIRs are untouched. Implementation: `tools/yaw_rotation.rotate_scene_yaw` (exp_03, reviewed) applied per sample through a batched variant with per-sample offsets.
- **Seeding (literal port of `_yaw_aug_step_seed`):** the per-micro-batch generator seed is `_yaw_aug_step_seed(seed, t, rank=0)` with the FLAC mixer copied verbatim (splitmix64 key → `k_lo`, `k_hi`; `v = (t << 12) | 0`; `fmix32(v ^ k_lo) ^ k_hi`; 32-bit result) and the xRIR counter **`t = (epoch − 1)·9261 + batch_idx`** (micro-batch index over the whole run; 9 261 micro-batches per epoch, so t < 111 132 < 2^20, injective; gradient accumulation does not enter the counter). Offsets are drawn from a dedicated CPU `torch.Generator` seeded with that value, one `randint` call per micro-batch; the global Python/NumPy/Torch RNG streams are never consumed. Golden vectors (independently computed by the round-2 reviewer from the FLAC implementation and pinned as literals in the tests): for seed = 0 and t ∈ {0, 1, 9261, 111131} the mixer gives {1254532375, 2068746652, 2466316700, 368013681} and the first W = 512 offsets are {474, 395, 358, 401}; a collision check over the complete armed domain (111 132 values, rank 0) asserts injectivity; boundary tests reject t = −1, t = 2^20, rank = −1 and 4096, epoch 0, batch_idx −1 and 9261, and non-int/bool inputs.
- **Resume: none for the reportable arm.** The trainer's checkpoints do not carry the sampler, worker or RNG states, and a mid-epoch resume restarts the epoch; counter-based offsets cannot repair that. Therefore `--yaw-aug 1` refuses `--resume` and forces `--save-every 0` (no mid-epoch checkpoints); an interrupted run is recorded as `_ABORTED_` and restarted from epoch 1. (The un-augmented exp_01 control was itself a single uninterrupted run.)
- **Where:** `train_xRIR_backbone.py::compute_loss` in `train_epoch` only; `test_epoch` and every evaluator never augment (tested with a spy). A banner `yaw_aug ENABLED W=512 seed=<s> counter=(epoch-1)*9261+batch_idx` is printed and flushed before the first optimizer step; its absence fails the launch gate.
- **Single intended algorithmic/config delta with disabled-path evidence — not bitwise trajectory pairing.** With `--yaw-aug 0` the training path is behaviourally identical to the exp_01 script (parity: same seed, same synthetic batch → bit-identical loss, gradients, buffers, and unchanged Python/NumPy/Torch RNG states). The recipe otherwise equals the control's launch (`cylvit_vs_simplevit_command.md`: `--num-shot 8 --batch-size 32 --accum-steps 2 --tf32 --epochs 12 --decay-epochs 3 --num-workers 12 --log-interval 50 --save-every 500 --seed 0`, defaults `--lr 1e-3 --weight-decay 1e-4 --lr-gamma 0.1 --max-len 9600`, environment `XRIR_DATA_PATH=/home/yixunhu/data_cache/AcousticRooms`, `OMP_NUM_THREADS=2`, one GPU), verified by **one normalised schema used everywhere (§2, §5, §6): `effective_args.json` = every trainer flag by its argparse name plus the environment keys `XRIR_DATA_PATH`, `PYTHONHASHSEED`, `OMP_NUM_THREADS`, `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES`**; the parity diff against the control's `args.json` + recorded launch environment is type-strict and excludes exactly `save_dir`, `yaw_aug`, `yaw_aug_seed`, `yaw_aug_width`, `no_save`, `save_every`, `epoch_ckpt_every` and `PYTHONHASHSEED` (unrecorded for the control), every excluded difference being listed in the notebook. Guards: `--yaw-aug 1` requires `resume is None` and `save_every == 0` exactly (negative and positive values refused; tested). Known operational differences, enumerated: the control ran with `save_every 500` (mid-epoch `last.pth` overwrites) and `epoch_ckpt_every 5` plus per-epoch snapshots taken by the exp_01 evaluation pipeline (files present in `ckpt/xRIR_simple_8_shot/`: trainer-written `epoch_005.pth`, `epoch_010.pth`, `best_epoch01.pth`, `best.pth`, `last.pth`, `snapshot_ep1_partial.pth`; pipeline snapshots `epoch_02.pth` … `epoch_12.pth`; the confirmatory comparator is `epoch_12.pth`); the treatment saves every epoch and never mid-epoch. Realization limitations: training-sample order comes from an unordered `set` of paths (no `PYTHONHASHSEED` recorded for the control; it is set and recorded for the treatment), references are drawn by worker-local `np.random.choice`, one training seed per arm — the two arms cannot share the exact sample/reference trajectory, and run-to-run training noise is not estimated. An exact causal single-delta claim would require a contemporaneous un-augmented retrain, which is not planned (cost); the claim made is the intended-delta one.
- **Alignment policy (fixed now):** the model's `shift_and_align` runs on the **rotated** geometry, exactly as at inference on any scene — no special-casing, no branch. Its integer delays are rounded float32 distances, so a rotation can flip a delay by one sample for a few pairs (exp_03: ≤ 4 of 50 696 pairs per angle; the gain `‖ref‖/(‖src‖ + 1e-7)` is continuous and ulp-scale changes are harmless, so only integer-delay changes are counted). Rung 4 measures, on a deterministic cohort — the first 50 micro-batches of epoch 1 with their actual counter offsets — the fraction of (query, reference) pairs whose integer delay differs between the rotated and the un-rotated geometry (denominator: all pairs in the cohort, 50 × 32 × 8), and the record discloses it as part of the treatment's definition. Descriptive only; no threshold selects a different training path.

## 3. Pre-registered hypotheses and decision rules

Estimand basis (adopting FLAC directive 04): every reported cell is the mean ± sample SD over **five evaluation seeds s ∈ {42, 43, 44, 45, 46}**; seed s selects the K-specific reference manifest (`build_manifest(seed=s, num_shot=K)`) and the Griffin-Lim phase seed (`--gl-seed s`). Control and treatment are evaluated under every seed with the same evaluator closure, so every comparison is paired per query and per seed. Inference: per query, the five per-seed errors of each arm are averaged with plain arithmetic means (never `nanmean`; a query with any non-finite value in a cell's mask is excluded from that cell); the paired per-query differences (or ratios inside the resample) are bootstrapped over queries (20 000 resamples, bootstrap seed 0; query-level, this fixed split) with the room-cluster bootstrap (17 rooms) as the secondary interval. **Convergence gate:** every decision-driving query-level bound is recomputed with bootstrap seed 1 on the companion interval whose upper endpoint *is* the decision bound — H1 [Q_0.025, Q_0.975], H2 [Q_0.00625, Q_0.99375], TOST [Q_{0.05/14}, Q_{1−0.05/14}] — and the maximum endpoint movement divided by the seed-0 width must be ≤ 0.10; otherwise no canonical artefact is written until `n_boot` is raised and the gate passes. Single-seed runs are technical diagnostics only and never enter a table or a verdict. H1, H2 and the TOST block are separate family-local claims; there is no omnibus success rule.

- **H1 (accuracy at the canonical heading; K = 8, unseen, epoch 12 vs epoch 12, condition P at k = 0).** For each j ∈ {EDT, C50}, ρ_j = (mean error_aug − mean error_control)/mean error_control (recomputed inside each resample). Per-cell mask: a query enters iff its value is finite for all five seeds in both arms at k = 0 (ordinary five-value means afterwards); an empty cell is refused, exclusions are reported per arm and seed. H0_j: ρ_j ≥ +0.03; HA_j: ρ_j < +0.03. Test statistic: the **one-sided upper bound U_j = Q_{1−0.05/2} = Q_{0.975}** of the bootstrap distribution of ρ_j (family m = 2, Bonferroni); H1 passes for metric j iff U_j < +0.03. Verdict: "non-inferior" (both pass), "non-inferior on EDT/C50 only", "not shown". Superiority is reported when the upper endpoint of the two-sided adjusted companion interval [Q_0.0125, Q_0.9875] (family 2) is below 0; that interval is included in the convergence gate. The same rule, as a separate family, at K = 1. T60 descriptive.
- **H2 (yaw robustness; K = 8, condition P).** D_k = r_k(aug) − r_k(control) on shared resamples at k ∈ {32, 64, 448, 480} (±22.5°, ±45°) for C50 and EDT (family m = 8, directional one-sided bound U = Q_{1−0.05/8} = Q_{0.99375}); a cell passes iff U < 0. **C50 is primary:** "supported" if all four C50 cells pass, "partially supported" if some, "not supported" if none; EDT cells are supportive (reported, not in the verdict). D_k at the other acoustic angles is descriptive only where both arms have five-seed data (none beyond the four H2 angles under this matrix); the augmented arm's own r_k at the remaining angles, and any comparison with exp_03's single-seed control sweep, are labelled single-seed diagnostics. Per-cell mask: a query enters cell (metric, k) only if its value is finite for every seed, both arms, at k = 0 and at k; invalidity counts are reported by category (baseline-invalid, newly invalid, per arm).
- **TOST (near-invariance of the augmented arm only; one-arm test; K = 8, condition P, five seeds).** r_k(aug) within ±2 % at the seven non-zero patch-aligned acoustic angles k ∈ {32, 64, 128, 256, 384, 448, 480} for C50 and EDT: family m = 14, each test at α = 0.05/14, two one-sided tests; per-cell mask: finite for every augmented-arm seed at k = 0 and k; wording "equivalent" / "equivalence not established".
- **Descriptive:** the augmented arm's absolute r_k curve (acoustic grid, seed 42, and the 18-angle spectral grid), the epoch-9 checkpoint (41 679 updates, the exp_17 "40k" convention) at K = 8 under the five seeds — diagnostic only, no verdict; no `best.pth` or test-selected epoch enters any verdict; no seen-split evaluation.
