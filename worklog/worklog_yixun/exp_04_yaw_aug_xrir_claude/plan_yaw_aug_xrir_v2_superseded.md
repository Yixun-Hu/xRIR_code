## 1. Question

## 2. Treatment and single-delta contract (port of exp_17 §2)

- **Augmentation semantics (port of FLAC `draw_yaw_offsets` / `offsets_to_radians` / `rotate_scene_metadata` and `_apply_yaw_aug`):** for every training sample, one integer panorama-column offset `d ~ Uniform{0, …, W−1}`, W = 512 (every sample rotated, no skip probability), exact angle `Δ = 2πd/W`, active yaw of the whole scene: `depth_coord' = Rz(Δ)·roll(depth_coord, d, dim=W)`, `src_loc' = Rz(Δ)·src_loc`, `ref_locs' = Rz(Δ)·ref_locs`. The target RIR and the reference RIRs are untouched. Implementation: `tools/yaw_rotation.rotate_scene_yaw` (exp_03, reviewed) applied per sample through a batched variant with per-sample offsets.
- **Seeding (literal port of `_yaw_aug_step_seed`):** the per-micro-batch generator seed is `_yaw_aug_step_seed(seed, t, rank=0)` with the FLAC mixer copied verbatim (splitmix64 key → `k_lo`, `k_hi`; `v = (t << 12) | 0`; `fmix32(v ^ k_lo) ^ k_hi`; 32-bit result) and the xRIR counter **`t = (epoch − 1)·9261 + batch_idx`** (micro-batch index over the whole run; 9 261 micro-batches per epoch, so t < 111 132 < 2^20, injective; gradient accumulation does not enter the counter). Offsets are drawn from a dedicated CPU `torch.Generator` seeded with that value, one `randint` call per micro-batch; the global Python/NumPy/Torch RNG streams are never consumed. Golden vectors: the seed for (seed = 0, t ∈ {0, 1, 9261, 111131}) and the first offsets, generated once from the FLAC implementation in the worktree and pinned as literals in the tests; a collision check over the complete armed domain (111 132 values, rank 0) asserts injectivity.
- **Resume: none for the reportable arm.** The trainer's checkpoints do not carry the sampler, worker or RNG states, and a mid-epoch resume restarts the epoch; counter-based offsets cannot repair that. Therefore `--yaw-aug 1` refuses `--resume` and forces `--save-every 0` (no mid-epoch checkpoints); an interrupted run is recorded as `_ABORTED_` and restarted from epoch 1. (The un-augmented exp_01 control was itself a single uninterrupted run.)
- **Where:** `train_xRIR_backbone.py::compute_loss` in `train_epoch` only; `test_epoch` and every evaluator never augment (tested with a spy). A banner `yaw_aug ENABLED W=512 seed=<s> counter=(epoch-1)*9261+batch_idx` is printed and flushed before the first optimizer step; its absence fails the launch gate.
- **Single intended algorithmic/config delta with disabled-path evidence — not bitwise trajectory pairing.** With `--yaw-aug 0` the training path is behaviourally identical to the exp_01 script (parity: same seed, same synthetic batch → bit-identical loss, gradients, buffers, and unchanged Python/NumPy/Torch RNG states). The recipe otherwise equals the control's `args.json` (backbone `simple`, `--batch-size 32 --accum-steps 2 --tf32`, 12 epochs, lr 1e-3, `--decay-epochs 3 --lr-gamma 0.1`, weight decay 1e-4, seed 0, 12 workers), verified by a type-strict diff of the two `args.json` files that excludes only `save_dir`, the three yaw keys and the checkpoint-cadence keys. Known operational differences, enumerated: the control ran with `save_every 500` (mid-epoch `last.pth` overwrites) and `epoch_ckpt_every 5` plus per-epoch snapshots taken by the exp_01 evaluation pipeline (files present in `ckpt/xRIR_simple_8_shot/`: trainer-written `epoch_005.pth`, `epoch_010.pth`, `best_epoch01.pth`, `best.pth`, `last.pth`, `snapshot_ep1_partial.pth`; pipeline snapshots `epoch_02.pth` … `epoch_12.pth`; the confirmatory comparator is `epoch_12.pth`); the treatment saves every epoch and never mid-epoch. Realization limitations: training-sample order comes from an unordered `set` of paths (no `PYTHONHASHSEED` recorded for the control; it is set and recorded for the treatment), references are drawn by worker-local `np.random.choice`, one training seed per arm — the two arms cannot share the exact sample/reference trajectory, and run-to-run training noise is not estimated. An exact causal single-delta claim would require a contemporaneous un-augmented retrain, which is not planned (cost); the claim made is the intended-delta one.
- **`shift_and_align` under rotation (numerical audit, pre-registered rule):** the alignment delays are integer-rounded functions of float32 distances, so a rotation can flip a delay by one sample for a few pairs (exp_03 saw ≤ 4 of 50 696 pairs per angle). Rung 4 measures the fraction of (query, reference) pairs whose integer delay or alignment gain changes under the assigned yaws on 50 real batches. Rule: if the flip fraction is ≤ 1 %, alignment keeps using the rotated geometry (the finite-precision effect is part of the treatment and is disclosed with its measured value); if > 1 %, the alignment is computed from the un-rotated geometry (`fixed_alignment` as in exp_03's condition P) and that choice is disclosed. The rule is fixed now, before the measurement.

## 3. Pre-registered hypotheses and decision rules

Estimand basis (adopting FLAC directive 04): every reported cell is the mean ± sample SD over **five evaluation seeds s ∈ {42, 43, 44, 45, 46}**; seed s selects the K-specific reference manifest (`build_manifest(seed=s, num_shot=K)`) and the Griffin-Lim phase seed (`--gl-seed s`). Control and treatment are evaluated under every seed with the same evaluator closure, so every comparison is paired per query and per seed. Inference: per query, the five per-seed errors of each arm are averaged; the paired per-query differences (or ratios inside the resample) are bootstrapped over queries (20 000 resamples; query-level, this fixed split) with the room-cluster bootstrap (17 rooms) as the secondary interval; the second-bootstrap-seed convergence gate of exp_03 applies (max endpoint movement ≤ 10 % of width). Single-seed runs are technical diagnostics only and never enter a table or a verdict. H1, H2 and the TOST block are separate family-local claims; there is no omnibus success rule.

- **H1 (accuracy at the canonical heading; K = 8, unseen, epoch 12 vs epoch 12, condition P at k = 0).** For each j ∈ {EDT, C50}, ρ_j = (mean error_aug − mean error_control)/mean error_control (recomputed inside each resample). H0_j: ρ_j ≥ +0.03; HA_j: ρ_j < +0.03. Test statistic: the **one-sided upper bound U_j = Q_{1−0.05/2} = Q_{0.975}** of the bootstrap distribution of ρ_j (family m = 2, Bonferroni); H1 passes for metric j iff U_j < +0.03. Verdict: "non-inferior" (both pass), "non-inferior on EDT/C50 only", "not shown". Superiority is reported when the two-sided adjusted upper bound is below 0. The same rule, as a separate family, at K = 1. T60 descriptive.
- **H2 (yaw robustness; K = 8, condition P).** D_k = r_k(aug) − r_k(control) on shared resamples at k ∈ {32, 64, 448, 480} (±22.5°, ±45°) for C50 and EDT (family m = 8, directional one-sided bound U = Q_{1−0.05/8} = Q_{0.99375}); a cell passes iff U < 0. **C50 is primary:** "supported" if all four C50 cells pass, "partially supported" if some, "not supported" if none; EDT cells are supportive (reported, not in the verdict). D_k at the other acoustic angles and the spectral angles is descriptive only. Every cell uses one common finite mask across both arms at k = 0 and k; invalidity counts are reported by category.
- **TOST (near-invariance of the augmented arm; K = 8, condition P).** r_k(aug) within ±2 % at the seven non-zero patch-aligned acoustic angles k ∈ {32, 64, 128, 256, 384, 448, 480} for C50 and EDT: family m = 14, each test at α = 0.05/14, two one-sided tests; wording "equivalent" / "equivalence not established".
- **Descriptive:** the augmented arm's absolute r_k curve (acoustic grid, seed 42, and the 18-angle spectral grid), the epoch-9 checkpoint (41 679 updates, the exp_17 "40k" convention) at K = 8 under the five seeds — diagnostic only, no verdict; no `best.pth` or test-selected epoch enters any verdict; no seen-split evaluation.
