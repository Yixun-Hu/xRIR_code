## 1. Question

Within one acoustic system (xRIR, K-shot RIR prediction) and one backbone family (a panorama ViT with the token count fixed at 256), does the CylindricalViT encoder (CERPA: per-column gauge alignment, elevation-only positional code, circular relative-position bias) reach lower unseen-room acoustic error than the SimpleViT encoder at every encoder parameter budget (experiment 2 of the user's note), and can a smaller cylindrical encoder match a larger baseline one (experiment 3)?

## 2. Arms: three matched tiers × two backbones

Only the encoder configuration changes; everything else in xRIR (AudioEnc ResNet-18, `lin_proj_0` token pool over 256 tokens, coordinate embeddings, `lin_proj_1/2`, decoder-side weighting) is identical across all six models. `src_proj` (dim → 256) scales with dim; it is counted in the full-system column only, and the encoder column is `source_network` alone. |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S | 256 | 6 | 4 | 256 | 2.77 M / 15.07 M | 2.78 M / 15.09 M |
| M (= exp_01) | 512 | 12 | 8 | 512 | 19.70 M / 32.08 M | 19.75 M / 32.12 M |

Each pair is matched by construction (the backbones differ only in the positional code): exact encoder counts (verified by the reviewer against the model definitions and the M checkpoints) S 2 766 080 vs 2 777 984 (+0.43 %), M 19 703 296 vs 19 750 912 (+0.24 %), L 43 709 184 vs 43 780 608 (+0.16 %) — **registered matching tolerance ≤ 0.5 %**, exact integers reported next to the rounded values; the tiers span 16× in encoder parameters. Trainable = parameters with `requires_grad` = full − 20 (the sinusoidal embedding's 20 frozen frequency buffers-as-parameters), reported as such; the encoder/full boundary is `source_network` vs the whole `xRIR` module. The M tier is exp_01's existing pair (`ckpt/xRIR_simple_8_shot/epoch_12.pth`, `ckpt/xRIR_cyl_8_shot/epoch_12.pth`) and is **not retrained**; four new runs: S-simple, S-cyl, L-simple, L-cyl.

**Single-delta contract per tier:** the two runs of a tier share the recipe, seed and step budget and differ only in `--backbone`; across tiers only `dim/depth/heads/mlp_dim` change. Same qualification as exp_04 §2: one training seed per run, non-shared data order/reference realizations (unordered-set path listing, worker-local `np.random.choice`), so this is a single intended delta with disabled-path evidence, not bitwise pairing; `PYTHONHASHSEED=0` is pinned for the new runs (unrecorded for exp_01's M runs; disclosed).

## 5. Pre-registered hypotheses and decision rules

Statistics as exp_03/exp_04: per query, the five per-seed errors are averaged (plain means; a query enters a cell only if finite for all seeds in both arms of the cell); paired per-query differences bootstrapped over queries (20 000 resamples, bootstrap seed 0; query-level primary, this fixed split) with the room-cluster bootstrap (17 rooms) secondary; two-seed convergence gate (max endpoint movement ≤ 10 % of the seed-0 width, else raise `n_boot`). Each hypothesis is a separate family; no omnibus rule.

- **H1 (dominance of the curve; K = 8, EDT and C50 error, epoch 12):** for every tier t ∈ {S, M, L}, δ_t = (mean error_cyl,t − mean error_simple,t) / mean error_simple,t (recomputed inside each paired resample). Family m = 6 (3 tiers × 2 metrics), two-sided adjusted intervals [Q_{0.05/12}, Q_{1−0.05/12}], bootstrap seeds 0/1 with the convergence gate. Verdict per metric: "dominance supported on <metric> at the three tested tiers" iff the adjusted upper bound of δ_t is below 0 at all three tiers; "partial" if at some; "not supported" if none — stated separately for EDT and C50, never as an unqualified "dominance", and never extrapolated between the tested capacities. T60 descriptive. The same table at K = 1 as a separate secondary family, **reported in full alongside K = 8** (all cells, intervals, cohorts and verdicts), with the analysis stating explicitly where the K = 1 conclusions differ from the K = 8 conclusions (exp_03's K = 1 follow-up showed the reversal at M; the curve tells whether it is tier-dependent). H2 is likewise computed and reported at K = 1 as a secondary four-cell family with the same rules.
- **H2 (fixed-target reading; experiment 3): one four-cell family** = {Cyl-S vs Base-M, Cyl-M vs Base-L} × {EDT, C50} at K = 8. The target of a cell is the baseline arm's five-seed mean of the metric **computed on that cell's registered paired cohort** (queries finite for all five seeds in both arms of the cell) — the same cohort on which ρ = (mean error_cyl − mean error_base)/mean error_base is recomputed inside each paired resample (bootstrap seeds 0 and 1). The "reaches the target" claim and any parameter-ratio statement are scoped to that cohort; the baseline's own-cohort curve mean is shown separately and labelled, and the producer reports both together with the cohort sizes (a producer test uses the reviewer's counterexample — baseline [1, 1, 3, 3], cylindrical [invalid, invalid, 2.4, 2.6] — to assert that the target is 3 on the paired cohort, not 2). "Reaches the target" iff the superiority interval [Q_0.00625, Q_0.99375] has its upper endpoint below 0, **or** TOST equivalence within ±3 % holds with each one-sided test at α = 0.0125, i.e. [Q_0.0125, Q_0.9875] strictly inside (−0.03, +0.03). The encoder-parameter ratio is reported for a pairing only if both its metric cells reach the target (2 777 984 vs 19 703 296 ≈ 7.1×; 19 750 912 vs 43 709 184 ≈ 2.2×). Every decision-driving interval is covered by the two-seed convergence gate. No other pairing is tested.
- **Cohorts (registered):** a plotted curve point, its seed SD and every fixed target use the arm's own cohort — the queries finite for all five seeds of that arm and metric; a comparison cell uses the paired cohort — queries finite for all five seeds in both arms of the cell. Both cohorts' sizes (queries and rooms) and the excluded queries per arm/seed/metric are reported; an empty cohort refuses the cell; where a plotted mean and a cell mean differ because of the cohorts, both are shown and labelled.
- **Descriptive:** the two curves (x = encoder parameters, log scale; y = error with seed SD and bootstrap intervals), the full-system parameter axis as a second panel, per-epoch test-loss trajectories, the yaw-robustness block per tier, throughput and memory per tier.

## 7. Validation ladder, parity audit, acceptance criteria

1. static; 2. `pytest tests/`; 3. tiny forward + backward at S and L on GPU 1 (full-model steps need a GPU: the baseline `apply_delay` hard-codes `.cuda()` — do not "fix" that here); 4. real-data readback: one real batch through each tier, shapes and loss finite; 5. **fit and timing probes for all four new arms at the intended recipe (32 × 2)**, storage-light (`--no-save` if landed, else a scratch save dir removed afterwards): 10 warm-up micro-batches then 50 timed micro-batches; peak memory and the mean iteration time are recorded; T_epoch = 9 261 × t_micro + t_test + t_save (t_micro = mean timed micro-batch time, t_test = measured test-epoch time, t_save = measured per-epoch save time) and T_run = 12 × T_epoch; launch only if every arm projects T_run ≤ 60 h; if L does not fit at 32 × 2, stop and amend the tier (§9); 6. parity: the M defaults reproduce both exp_01 models — state-dict keys/shapes identical to both M checkpoints, and for each backbone a fixed-seed step on a real batch gives loss, gradients, buffers and RNG states bit-identical to the pre-change script (`git show <base>:train_xRIR_backbone.py`); a type-strict diff of one **normalised schema** against the M runs' `args.json` and recorded launch environment: every trainer flag by its argparse name plus the environment keys (`XRIR_DATA_PATH`, `PYTHONHASHSEED`, `OMP_NUM_THREADS`, `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES`); fields absent from the historical M records are normalised to their explicitly checked defaults before comparison (the tier flags → the M values; exp_04's `yaw_aug/yaw_aug_seed/yaw_aug_width/no_save`, if present, → 0/None/512/false, with a nonzero `yaw_aug` refused outright); derived fields (`param_counts`, `env`, `len(train_loader)`) are validated separately, not diffed; the allowlist of permitted differences is exactly `save_dir`, `save_every`, `epoch_ckpt_every`, `PYTHONHASHSEED` (unrecorded for M) and `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES` (GPU placement), each listed in the notebook; 7. launch. Acceptance criteria per launch (in the notebook before launching): commit SHA, GPU, exact argv, ≥ 1 finite optimizer step, epoch-1 time ≤ 1.05 × T_epoch of that arm's probe (on failure: stop the run, record it as `_ABORTED_slow`, and re-probe before any relaunch), `history.jsonl` per epoch, `epoch_NNN.pth` for every epoch, no resume.

## 9. Cost, risks, decisions

- Cost ≈ 110–140 GPU-hours training (probe-refined) + evaluation ≈ 12.5 h: the 60 standalone k = 0 runs ≈ 8 h (this already includes the M pair's 2 arms × 2 K × 5 seeds = 20 runs ≈ 2 h 40 min, deducted only if exp_04's M outputs pass the admission checks) plus six seed-42 yaw blocks ≈ 4.5 h; ≈ 3 days on two A6000s after exp_04's training frees GPU 0.
- Risks: **micro-batch 32 × accumulation 2 is mandatory for every confirmatory arm** (AudioEnc's BatchNorm statistics and the trainer's per-accumulation loss scaling, including the final incomplete group, depend on the micro-batch; a 16 × 4 fallback would break the cross-tier contract). If the L tier does not fit at 32 × 2 in the fit probe, L is redesigned to a smaller registered configuration (e.g. dim 640, depth 12, heads 10, mlp 640) before any launch and the plan is amended; S may under-fit within 12 epochs (same budget is the design; the trajectory is reported); one training seed per arm; the M pair predates this experiment (different day, unrecorded `PYTHONHASHSEED`) — disclosed as an operational difference.
- Decisions (taken by the user, 2026-09-12): (1) tiers S/M/L only — no S+ this round; (2) reuse exp_01's M pair; the analysis must disclose clearly the historical-run differences (different day, launcher v1 cadence, unrecorded `PYTHONHASHSEED`, evaluations reused only after the admission checks) and the limitation that training realizations are not exactly paired (one seed per arm, unshared data order and reference draws); (3) K = 8 primary, K = 1 secondary, both reported completely with any differences in conclusions stated; (4) the two fixed-target comparisons Cyl-S vs Base-M and Cyl-M vs Base-L, testing whether the smaller cylindrical model reaches comparable (TOST ±3 %) or better (superiority) accuracy under the pre-registered criteria.
