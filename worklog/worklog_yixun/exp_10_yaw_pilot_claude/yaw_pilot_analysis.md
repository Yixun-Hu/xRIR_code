# Analysis — exp_10 yaw_pilot

**Planner:** Claude Fable 5.1 · **Date:** 2026-09-27 · **Canonical data:** `ckpt/exp10/summary/yaw_pilot_summary.json` (four GPU arms, 10 000 bootstrap replicates, seeds 0/1, 95 % intervals; sha256 `826498f58780dfe42a4e6228075b0fcb2be845a18a0986d0e835302faee85b32`) and `ckpt/exp10/cpu_protocol/summary/yaw_pilot_summary.json` (the released K = 8 CPU protocol; sha256 `3526094b796a9c1f8bd4d8bae82f8373a7df063ff68cfec6fecdc601f1b8ced1`); every number below is copied from those files or from the run reports they bind. `yaw_pilot_results.md` / `yaw_pilot_01_results.html` carry the full tables, generated by the Codex-reviewed tooling (`yaw_pilot_results_assets/`, assets under `generated/` with `SHA256SUMS`).

## 1. What was measured

The xRIR counterpart of FLAC's exp_02 pilot: on the AcousticRooms unseen split (6 337 queries, 17 rooms) the receiver-frame depth panorama and every source / reference-source coordinate are rotated in yaw by 45°, 90°, 180° and 270° while the reference audio, the target and the direct-path alignment (condition P) stay fixed — a transformation under which the true RIR does not change. Two readouts per arm, angle and metric, on the shared comparison mask, with paired query-level and room-cluster bootstrap intervals:

- **Δ** — signed mean change of the error vs ground truth (α minus 0°): EDT (s), C50 (dB), T60 (percentage points of the GT-normalised error, plus the absolute-seconds supplement);
- **G** — mean shift of the prediction at α relative to the prediction at 0°, without ground truth: EDT (s), C50 (dB), T60 (% of the baseline prediction's T60, plus absolute seconds), waveform rel-L2 / MAD, and the Griffin-Lim-free spectral readout (log-spectrogram MAD, magnitude rel-L2).

Arms: the released checkpoint at K = 8 (primary; xRIR's paper setting), the released checkpoint "trained K = 8, evaluated K = 1", the same-budget SimpleViT control and the CylindricalViT (exp_01, epoch 12). All four on GPU 1 (A6000), batch 16 with canonical padding, TF32 off, per-query seeded Griffin-Lim (gl_seed 0), metrics on the first 8 000 samples.

## 2. Reliability

- **Controls** (probe, every arm): fresh k = 0 repeat, fresh k = 128 repeat and k = 512 (full turn) reproduce their originals exactly — max |Δ| = 0 on waveforms, log-spectrograms and acoustic metrics.
- **Replication of exp_03** (same manifests, seeds and batch shape): `released_k8`, `control_k8` and `cyl_k8` replicate exp_03's GPU sweeps on all 6 337 queries at every angle with zero difference on EDT / C50 / T60 and the loss terms and ≤ 1.5e-7 on the log-spectrogram consistency, with identical validity masks (probe and full split; `generated/runs/<arm>/parity_exp03_{probe,all}.json`, every cell status `replication`). `released_k1` has no predecessor (controls only).
- **Offline reproduction:** `check_online` OK on every run — the waveform and acoustic gaps recomputed from the saved float32 arrays equal the online values exactly; the spectral gaps are online-only by contract.
- **Backend sensitivity:** the released K = 8 arm was first run on CPU (405 min; both GPUs were occupied). Its acoustic cells pass exp_03's per-query tolerances (criteria b, c, identical masks), but 7–9 of 6 337 queries differ from the GPU values in log-spectrogram MAD by up to 0.0095 (criterion a, ≤ 1e-5 per query, fails), so by the pre-registered rule (plan §7, v3.1) the CPU run is a **separate CPU protocol** — it did not replicate exp_03 as a whole — and it is kept apart from the four primary arms; the arm was re-run on GPU 1 once the card was free and the GPU run is the primary. The side-by-side table (`generated/backend_sensitivity_released_k8.md`) shows small point differences (largest relative difference 0.46 %, e.g. EDT 45° Δ −0.5848 ms GPU vs −0.5821 ms CPU; intervals overlapping to the third digit) and the same query-level denominator status in all 16 EDT / C50 / T60 / T60-absolute cells. This is a measured similarity on one arm, not backend equivalence. (Both CPU-side launcher failures, the K = 1 hash bug and the edited-while-running queue script are disclosed in the worklog; none touched a run's data.)
- **Convergence:** every interval was recomputed with seed 1 (10 metrics × 4 angles × 4 arms = 160 non-zero-angle cells). Two headline cells are withheld as "unresolved Monte Carlo uncertainty", for two different reasons: `cyl_k8`, T60, 270° (the ratio bound moved by 0.109 of its width, above the 0.10 limit; both seeds say "improvement") and `control_k8`, T60-absolute, 45° (seed statuses disagree: "undefined" under seed 0, whose draws contain exact-zero Δ* at a fraction of 1e-4, vs "denominator uncertain" under seed 1). The tables disclose both; the figures' per-angle abbreviation is corrected in the round-8 regeneration to `mc?` for these cells (before it showed the query-level status).
- **Pipeline qualification:** waveform and acoustic gaps measure the sensitivity of the model-plus-Griffin-Lim pipeline conditional on the per-query phase seed; Griffin-Lim can amplify a magnitude change. The GL-free spectral readout is reported beside them everywhere.

## 3. Findings (descriptive; statuses and wording per plan §1 / §12)

**3.1 The prediction moves a lot under a conditioning rotation — every arm, every angle.** Released K = 8: EDT gap 23.8–26.3 ms, C50 gap 0.59–0.64 dB, T60 gap 3.1–3.8 % (18–22 ms absolute), log-spectrogram MAD 0.157–0.180, magnitude rel-L2 0.20–0.23, waveform rel-L2 0.82–0.88; the query-level gap intervals are narrow (about ± 5 %), the room-cluster intervals are much wider (released K = 8 EDT 45°: 26.2 ms [10.6, 43.7]). The exp_01 models shift less (control: EDT 10.1–16.4 ms, C50 0.30–0.44 dB, T60 1.5–2.5 %; cylindrical: EDT 7.8–14.3 ms, C50 0.23–0.38 dB, T60 1.2–2.3 %); the released checkpoint evaluated at K = 1 (trained at K = 8) has EDT gaps of 10.6–12.5 ms (below both K = 8 exp_01 arms at 45°, above both at the other angles) and is lowest on C50 / T60 at 45°, 90° and 270° (0.21–0.23 dB, 1.3–1.5 %) but not at 180°, where its C50 shift peaks (0.245 dB) and the cylindrical model is lower (0.235 dB, T60 1.23 % vs 1.44 %). For the K = 8 arms the shift is largest at 45° on every metric (the diagonal heading, as in exp_03) and similar at 90°, 180° and 270°.

**3.2 The net accuracy change is small and, for the headline arm, mostly not distinguishable from zero — or an improvement.** Released K = 8 (query-level / room-level status): EDT Δ = −0.6 ms [−1.8, +0.6] at 45° and −0.9 [−2.0, +0.1] at 270° (denominator uncertain / uncertain), −1.6 [−2.7, −0.5] at 90° and −1.2 [−2.4, −0.06] at 180° (improvement at the query level; room level uncertain); C50 Δ = +0.033 dB [+0.006, +0.060] at 45° (defined / uncertain), −0.003, +0.008, +0.022 dB elsewhere (uncertain / uncertain); T60 Δ = +0.47 pp [+0.32, +0.62] at 45° (defined / defined), +0.26 at 90° and +0.16 at 270° (defined / uncertain), −0.24 at 180° (improvement / uncertain).

**3.4 Improvements and deteriorations largely cancel — shown by the paired contributions, not inferred from the ratio.** For the released K = 8 arm at 45°, 50.4 % of queries get a worse EDT, 47.3 % a better one and 2.3 % are unchanged (3 195 / 2 998 / 144 of 6 337); the mean positive part of e_α − e_0 is +10.9 ms and the mean negative part −11.4 ms, netting to −0.6 ms; C50: 52.5 % worse, 47.5 % better, none unchanged, +0.29 / −0.26 dB → +0.03 dB; T60: 53.0 % worse, 46.5 % better, 0.6 % unchanged, +2.0 / −1.5 pp → +0.47 pp. The same holds at the other angles (fractions worse 47–53 %, unchanged 0–3 %). Individual predictions therefore change by roughly the size of the prediction shift, in both directions.

**3.5 Cross-arm remarks (descriptive).** The cylindrical model shifts less than the same-budget control on every metric and angle — by 13–23 % on the EDT gap (14.3 vs 16.4 ms at 45°), 14–24 % on C50, 6.5–20 % on T60 and 10–16 % on the log-spectrogram MAD (0.114 vs 0.127 at 45°) — but its EDT error rises at all four angles (+0.4 to +1.15 ms, query-level defined; room-level defined at 45° and 90°), whereas the control's EDT change is defined only at 90° — consistent with exp_03's paired k = 0 / rotation results. The control's T60 error *improves* at 90°, 180° and 270° (−0.12 to −0.34 pp). At K = 1 the released checkpoint's C50 error rises at every angle (+0.009 to +0.047 dB, all query-level defined, room-level uncertain) while its EDT improves at 90° and 180°.

## 4. The pilot-section statement, and its FLAC comparability

FLAC exp_02 (released FLAC_EMA, unseen K = 1, single runs, no intervals): accuracy degradation vs shift — EDT +3.5 / +6.3 / +4.0 ms vs 18.7 / 20.1 / 20.0 ms (shift-to-degradation 5.4× / 3.2× / 5.0×), T60 +0.39 / +0.73 / +0.45 pp vs 3.3–3.4 %, C50 +0.03 / +0.14 / +0.08 dB vs 0.55–0.60 dB at 90 / 180 / 270°. xRIR released K = 8 (this experiment, intervals above): EDT −1.6 / −1.2 / −0.9 ms vs 24.9 / 25.6 / 23.8 ms, T60 +0.26 / −0.24 / +0.16 pp vs 3.2 / 3.3 / 3.1 %, C50 −0.003 / +0.008 / +0.022 dB vs 0.61 / 0.64 / 0.59 dB. The shift magnitudes are similar across the two families (EDT ≈ 20–26 ms, C50 ≈ 0.6 dB, T60 ≈ 3.3 %), while xRIR's signed net accuracy change is smaller than FLAC's degradation and mixed in sign. Wording that the evidence supports, with its scope stated: *in both families a conditioning yaw rotation, which changes nothing physically, shifts the prediction substantially — EDT by about 20–26 ms, C50 by about 0.6 dB, T60 by about 3.3 % of the baseline prediction's T60 — while the net error against ground truth changes far less. FLAC's single-run EDT shifts are 3.2–5.4× its EDT degradations (T60 4.6–8.6×, C50 3.9–20.9×, without intervals). For xRIR's released checkpoint at K = 8, of the twelve primary cells (EDT, C50, T60 × four angles) five have a net change not resolvable from zero, three show a net improvement, and four show a net deterioration with a reportable shift-to-change multiple: T60 45° 8.0× [6.1, 11.6] (room-level status defined), T60 90° 12.3× [8.3, 24.3], T60 270° 19.2× [10.6, 71.6] and C50 45° 19.5× [10.3, 85.0] (room-level denominators uncertain).* No universal "order of magnitude" statement is licensed: multiples exist only for named reportable cells, and the room-level caveat travels with them.

Qualifications that must accompany any cross-family sentence: (i) xRIR's waveform / acoustic gaps include the Griffin-Lim inversion (§2); (ii) both evaluators use the first 8 000 samples at 22 050 Hz (FLAC `src/metrics/metric_callback.py:114`), but FLAC's AcousticRooms path keeps every T60 measurement in its mean, including failed ones carrying the helper's sentinel (`src/metrics/modules/RT60.py`, `update`: no invalid mask for AcousticRooms), whereas xRIR returns NaN and excludes the query; FLAC's C50 adds 1e-10 inside the log10 (`src/metrics/modules/C50.py`), xRIR's `Evaluator` does not — the acoustic gaps are conceptually, not numerically, comparable; (iii) no waveform-distance ranking across families (10 240 decoded samples vs 9 600 inverted samples with 21 padding samples); (iv) FLAC's arm is K = 1 with its own protocol, xRIR's primary is K = 8; xRIR's K = 1 arm is a K = 8-trained model evaluated with one reference; (v) FLAC's numbers are single evaluations without intervals.

## 5. Must-not-claim

Superiority or inferiority of any backbone (descriptive pairings only); invariance or equivalence from a zero-crossing Δ interval; any multiple for a cell whose `headline.reportable` is false (including the two "unresolved Monte Carlo uncertainty" cells — the withholding may not be bypassed by quoting the point ratio); a headline multiple for a cell whose room-level status is not "defined" without saying so; a universal "order of magnitude" or "predictions move ≈ N× more" statement across cells or families; cancellation without the paired contributions of §3.4; backend equivalence (the CPU run is a separate protocol; §2 shows a measured similarity on one arm); attributing the waveform / acoustic gaps to the model alone (they include Griffin-Lim); causal explanations of K = 1 vs K = 8 differences by the reference count (the K = 1 arm is a K = 8-trained model evaluated with one reference); numerical equality or a waveform-distance ranking of FLAC's and xRIR's metrics; anything about condition E, other angles, training, or seeds other than the recorded ones. All statements are scoped to the fixed checkpoints, reference manifests, phase seed and split.
