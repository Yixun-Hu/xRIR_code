## 1. Question and stance

**Question (Yixun, 2026-09-26).** Mimicking FLAC's exp_02 pilot: when the *conditioning* of the released xRIR checkpoint is yaw-rotated on the AcousticRooms unseen split, how much does the **prediction itself move** (Metric 1, P_α vs P_0, no GT) compared with how much **accuracy vs GT changes** (Metric 2)? FLAC's finding: "predictions move ~5× more than net accuracy loses".

**Stance.** A **descriptive pilot**, like FLAC's: paired means with query-level bootstrap intervals; no confirmatory hypotheses (exp_03 already ran the Bonferroni-adjusted H1/H2 tests on Metric 2 for K = 8). Pre-registered readouts, per arm, angle and metric:

- **R1** signed accuracy change Δ(α) = mean(e_α − e_0) vs GT, and prediction shift G(α) = mean(g_α), each with a 95 % paired query-level percentile bootstrap interval, on the **shared comparison mask** of §4; paired-valid count and exclusions reported.
- **R2** the ratio R = G/Δ, from the **same resamples**, with an explicit **status**: `defined` (Δ interval entirely positive), `improvement` (Δ interval entirely negative — reported as "predictions shift while net error improves", never as a positive multiple), `denominator uncertain` (Δ interval contains 0 — no ratio interval is reported, and this is **not** equivalence or invariance), `undefined` (any resample with Δ* exactly 0; the fraction of such draws is reported; draws are never discarded, no epsilon, no |Δ|). The FLAC-style statement "shift ≫ change" requires status `defined` and a ratio lower bound > 2. Ratios only for metrics whose error and gap share a definition (EDT seconds, C50 dB; T60 is qualified, §4).
- **R3** the **GL-free spectral shift** (`logspec_mad`, `mag_rel_l2`) reported beside the waveform/acoustic shift, and labelled: waveform and acoustic gaps are the sensitivity of the **model-plus-Griffin-Lim pipeline** conditional on the per-query phase seed (shared phase initialisation removes initialisation noise; Griffin-Lim's nonlinear response to magnitude changes remains and **can amplify** differences). No FLAC/xRIR *waveform-distance* ranking is made (FLAC's rel-L2 is over 10 240 directly decoded samples; xRIR's over 9 600 samples of an inverted magnitude, 21 of them padding); the FLAC-comparable quantities are the acoustic gaps under the same "P_0 as GT" convention, and only after the two evaluators' windows are checked in the analysis (both are believed to use the first 8 000 samples at 22 050 Hz; verified before any caption states it).

## 2. Mechanism recap (exp_03; unchanged)

Geometry enters `xRIR.forward` through the ViT views `(loc − depth_coord)/5` and the sinusoidal embedding of raw xyz. Active yaw by k columns (W = 512): `depth' = Rz(2πk/W)·roll(depth, k)`, `src' = Rz·src`, `ref_locs' = Rz·ref_locs`; reference audio and target unchanged (`tools/yaw_rotation.rotate_scene_yaw`, oracle-tested in exp_03). Condition **P** pins the direct-path alignment at k = 0 (`tools/yaw_rotation.fixed_alignment`) so only the geometry branches see the rotation — the analogue of FLAC's `--rotate-deg`, which rotates the conditioning and leaves the audio untouched. exp_03 measured E (end-to-end alignment) = P to 2e-5 **at K = 8**; E is not run here. The model outputs a log-magnitude `out` `[F=63, T=310]`; the magnitude is `M = exp(out) − 1e-8` (built on the inference device, then moved to CPU exactly as exp_03's `acoustic_metrics_batch` does) and the waveform is Griffin-Lim on CPU (`tools/per_sample_metrics.griffin_lim_seeded`, 32 iterations, phase seeded by `sample_seed(gl_seed, query)`), 9 579 samples zero-padded to 9 600.

## 4. Metrics (per query; NaN = invalid, never imputed)

**Metric 2 — accuracy vs GT at angle α (condition P):** `edt_err` (s), `c50_err` (dB), `t60_err` (% of T60(GT)) via `tools.per_sample_metrics.acoustic_metrics(pred = w_α, gt = GT)` (exp_03's validity rules, 8 000-sample window); `log_mse` and the test `loss` via `per_sample_losses`. **Raw measures** are also stored per angle (`edt_pred`, `c50_pred`, `t60_pred` and once `edt_gt`, `c50_gt`, `t60_gt`, from the same `Evaluator` calls, NaN under the same exceptions) so any normalisation can be recomputed offline; a test checks that the canonical error equals the error rebuilt from the raw measures wherever both are finite.

**Metric 1 — prediction shift, P_α vs P_0, no GT:**
- `wave_rel_l2` = ‖w_α − w_0‖₂ / (‖w_0‖₂ + 1e-8) and `wave_mad` = mean|w_α − w_0| over the stored 9 600-sample float32 waveforms (FLAC's `compare_predictions.waveform_gap` definitions; window difference stated in §1);
- `mag_rel_l2` = ‖M_α − M_0‖_F / ‖M_0‖_F with `M = exp(out) − 1e-8`, and `logspec_mad` = mean|out_α − out_0| (**= exp_03's `consistency`**, computed on the model's log-magnitude output, not a fresh log of M);
- acoustic gaps `edt_gap` (s), `c50_gap` (dB), `t60_gap` (% of T60(w_0)) = `acoustic_metrics(pred = w_α, gt = w_0)` — FLAC's "P_ref as GT" convention; plus the absolute supplement `t60_gap_abs` = |T60(w_α) − T60(w_0)| (s) and `t60_err_abs` = |T60(w_α) − T60(GT)| (s) from the raw measures.

**T60 normalisations.** ΔT60 is a **percentage-point change in the GT-normalised error**; G_T60 is a **percent of the baseline prediction's T60**; their ratio is reported only with that qualification, and the absolute-seconds pair (`t60_err_abs`, `t60_gap_abs`) gives the same-unit comparison. EDT (s) and C50 (dB) are same-unit by construction.

**Paired-bootstrap contract (per arm, angle, metric).** Comparison mask = queries where `e_0`, `e_α` and `g_α` are all finite (for spectral metrics the mask is all queries). One draw of query indices per replicate (10 000 replicates, seed 0, 95 % two-sided percentile); from the same draw compute Δ* = mean(e_α − e_0), G* = mean(g_α), R* = G*/Δ*. Report n_mask and the exclusion counts by cause (invalid at 0, at α, gap invalid). A standalone G on the broader "g_α finite" population is reported separately and labelled as such. Ratio status per R2. **Room-cluster bootstrap** (secondary): resample the 17 rooms with replacement, keep every query of a drawn room with the drawn multiplicity, recompute the query-weighted means (no per-room averaging). **Convergence:** every reported bound recomputed with seed 1; movement < 10 % of the interval width. **Historical variability band** (figure only): exp_04's five-seed SD of the same-budget SimpleViT at K = 8 (EDT 0.186 ms, C50 0.0053 dB, T60 0.009 %) / K = 1 (0.609 ms, 0.0133 dB, 0.101 %) and the cylindrical SDs for `cyl_k8` — labelled "historical baseline evaluation variability (references and phases redrawn), context only"; it plays no role in any decision or acceptance; the paired CIs are the uncertainty statement.

## 7. Validation ladder, controls, parity gate, acceptance

Static checks → new tests + `tests/test_exp03_record_tools.py` (pins unchanged) + the exp_04/exp_07 record suites → smoke (`--batches 0`, CPU) → **probe** on GPU 1: `released_k8` on the 17 room-spanning canonical batches (272 queries; identical batch composition to the full run, so its per-sample values are a subset of the full run's) at all five angles, with timing of inference / inversion / metrics / writing separately and a thread budget of 16 torch threads, one process; **controls** on the same probe: `ctrl_full_turn` (k = 512; identity path through `rotate_scene_yaw`, plumbing only), `ctrl_zero_repeat` (fresh k = 0 inference under its own id), `ctrl_k128_repeat` (fresh nonzero repeat) — all three must match their originals to 1e-6 (waveforms, log-spec) and 1e-9 (acoustic metrics). **Parity gate vs exp_03** (`sweep_released/per_sample_yaw.json`, same GPU class, TF32 off, same manifest / gl_seed / batch shape) on the probe queries, per angle in {0, 64, 128, 256, 384}: (a) per-sample `logspec_mad` |Δ| ≤ 1e-5 for every query; (b) per-sample `edt_err` / `c50_err` / `t60_err` |Δ| ≤ 1e-4 s / 1e-3 dB / 0.01 % for ≥ 99.5 % of queries (Griffin-Lim can amplify float noise for a few); (c) the **paired Δ on the probe queries** from the new run and from exp_03 agree within max(5 % of |Δ_exp03|, 2e-5 s / 2e-4 dB / 2e-3 %) and have the same sign, for every angle and metric. If the GPU probe fails (c), the launch is blocked and the cause investigated; if only the CPU fallback is available and fails, the run proceeds as a **separate CPU protocol** with Metric 2 quoted from exp_03 and the discrepancy tabulated. `control_k8` / `cyl_k8` run the same probe gate against `sweep_control` / `sweep_cyl`; `released_k1` runs the controls only (no predecessor). Each arm's full run is forecast from its own probe timing (K = 1 and K = 8 forward costs differ). **Full-run acceptance:** 6 337 queries in canonical order, every angle present, `meta.complete`, `check_online` passes, EDT/C50 finite counts at k = 0 equal exp_03's for the replicated arms, waveform arrays' sha256s recorded, worklog acceptance entry written before launch.
